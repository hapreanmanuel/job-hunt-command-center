Privacy

Last updated: 5 November 2026

Job Hunt Command Center is run by ("I"). This page says what is kept, why, and what you can do about it. Questions: .

What is kept

  • What you write: your jobs, listings, salaries, rounds and debriefs.
  • Your account: an id derived from the account you sign in with, and when it was created.

Signing in

You sign in with GitHub or Google, which confirms who you are. The app uses your GitHub username, or your Google email address, to check that you're invited. It uses your account number from either one to work out the id above, and keeps only that id. No email address or username is stored in the database.

To let you in, I keep your GitHub username or email address in the app's hosting settings, and for Google, in its list of testers. If you used the access request form, your answers stay in Google Forms.

Cookies and logs

One cookie keeps you signed in. It's strictly necessary, so no consent banner is needed, and the sign-in ends after at most 14 days without use. There are no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and your data is never sold or shared.

The app logs its own errors. Servers see your IP address, as with any website, and the hosting providers may log it for security.

Who can see it

  • Other users: no. Everything you write is kept apart from their data.
  • Me: I can read the database. I only look at someone's data to fix a problem or answer their request.

Who handles it for me

  • Render runs the app, in Frankfurt, Germany.
  • Supabase runs the database, in Frankfurt, Germany.
  • GitHub and Google handle sign-in, and Google the access request form.
  • Buy Me a Coffee (with Stripe for payments) handles donations, under its own privacy policy. It shows me what a supporter chooses to share, which I use only to say thanks and keep as long as tax law requires.

Outside the EU

Your data is stored in Germany. Render, Supabase, GitHub and Google are US companies, so US authorities may be able to ask them for what they handle. Such transfers rely on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework or the EU's standard contractual clauses.

Why (legal bases, for the GDPR)

  • To give you the service you asked for: your account and your data (contract).
  • To keep it safe and working, and to answer your mail (legitimate interest).

Home sorts your jobs by what needs you soonest. That only arranges your own list: no automated decisions are made about you, and no profile of you is built.

How long it's kept

  • Your account and data: until you delete them, or until the service ends.
  • Your username or email address in the invitation lists: until your access ends.
  • Mail you send me: as long as I need it to answer you.

I keep no backups of your data, and the free plans include none. What you delete is gone at once, and if the service loses your data, it can't be restored.

Your rights

Download my data gives you a copy at any time. Delete my data removes your account and everything in it straight away, and signs you out everywhere.

You can also ask me for a copy of your data, or to correct, erase or restrict it, or object to how it's used. I'll answer within a month. You can complain to a data protection authority: mine is Romania's ANSPDCP (dataprotection.ro), or use the one where you live in the EU.

Changes

If this page changes, the date at the top changes too. Important changes will be announced in the app.
